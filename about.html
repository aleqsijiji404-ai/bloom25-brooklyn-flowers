About Us

Rooted in Brooklyn,
Blooming with Love

Est. 2024 · Brooklyn, NY

A Passion for
Beautiful Flowers

Bloom & Co was born from a simple belief: that flowers have the power to transform any moment into something extraordinary. From our cozy studio on 25th Ave in Brooklyn, we craft each arrangement with care, creativity, and an deep love for natural beauty.

Every bouquet we create tells a story — of celebration, of love, of comfort, of gratitude. We source only the freshest flowers, delivered to us daily from trusted growers, ensuring that each arrangement arrives at your door looking as stunning as the day it was cut.

Brooklyn is our home, and our community is our heart. We're proud to serve our neighbors, from Park Slope to Bay Ridge, from Williamsburg to Bensonhurst — delivering joy, one flower at a time.
